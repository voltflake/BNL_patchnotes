## Defensive Perks
### Dig that health
- Combined both dig that health and dig that ammo into one perk, including both downsides
- Buffed both parts of the perk to 2025 values
- Increase from gained ammo from 5% to 15% (This is in line with 2025)
- Decreased ammo pool from 10% to 20% (This is in line with 2025)
- Increased healing from 10% to 20% (This is in line with 2025)
- Decreased bricks gained from 10% to 20%  (This is in line with 2025)
### Greedy
- Income is increased by 20% for ALL sources, including objectives and supply drops.
### Killer Bricks
- Increase bricks gained from 40% to 70% a kill
- Kills also generate 50 bricks for your teammates
- Keeps original downside
### Toe, Ball, Heel, Knee 
- The perk felt underwhelming and a buff was asked for.
- Changed fall damage reduction from 60% to 70%
